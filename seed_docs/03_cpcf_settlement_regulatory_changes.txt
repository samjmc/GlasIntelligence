COMMUNITY PHARMACY CONTRACTUAL FRAMEWORK (CPCF) SETTLEMENT 2024/25 AND 2025/26
Source: Community Pharmacy England, Gov.uk

FUNDING SETTLEMENT
The 2025/26 settlement provides:
- £3.073 billion baseline funding (19.7% increase over 2023/24)
- £215 million specifically for Pharmacy First services
- Total uplift represents over 30% compared to 2023/24 levels

Community Pharmacy England describes this as the largest funding increase in community pharmacy history, but pharmacy owners argue it does not cover increased costs including National Insurance contributions, minimum wage rises, energy costs, and medicine price inflation.

CONTRACTUAL EASEMENTS (BURDEN REDUCTION)
Several administrative requirements are being removed to reduce pharmacy workload:
- Patients no longer need to sign prescription declarations
- Staff reference requirements for NHS services removed
- Practice leaflet requirement removed
- Clinical audit requirement suspended for 2025/26
- Pharmacies only need to participate in 2 national health campaigns plus 2 ICB-selected campaigns
- Core opening hours can be amended more flexibly

DISTANCE SELLING PHARMACY (DSP) RESTRICTIONS
- DSPs will no longer be able to provide Advanced and Enhanced services on pharmacy premises from October 2025
- Remote consultations remain possible for all pharmacies including DSPs
- This change targets online pharmacy operators who were providing face-to-face services

NEW MEDICINES SERVICE (NMS) CHANGES
- NMS cannot be subcontracted to another provider
- Prevents NMS being provided via remote consultation with off-site pharmacists not employed by the pharmacy owner

COMMUNITY PHARMACY PRESCRIBING SERVICE (FROM APRIL 2026)
- Negotiations on a national community pharmacy prescribing service begin April 2026
- The Independent Prescribing Pathfinder Programme ended December 2025
- ICBs encouraged to continue local pathfinder sites with £1,500 per site payment
- NHS England committed to publishing national evaluation from University of Manchester
- Community Pharmacy England chief executive Janet Morrison warned a new prescribing service would be "difficult without new money"
- Over 30,000 consultations took place in pathfinder pilots, two-thirds for minor ailments

2026/27 NEGOTIATIONS
- DHSC entered consultation with Community Pharmacy England on the 2026/27 contractual framework in February 2026
- Key issues include: sustainable funding, workforce, digital transformation, and the role of clinical services
