NHS PHARMACY FIRST SERVICE - PAYMENT CAPS FROM MARCH 2026
Source: NHS Business Services Authority (NHSBSA)

OVERVIEW
The NHS Pharmacy First Service (PFS) replaced the Community Pharmacist Consultation Service (CPCS) and enables community pharmacies to treat seven common conditions through clinical pathways: uncomplicated urinary tract infections in women (16-64), shingles (18+), impetigo (1+), infected insect bites (1+), earache (1-17), sore throat (5+), and sinusitis (12+).

Pharmacists can supply NHS medicines where clinically appropriate, including some prescription-only medicines under Patient Group Directions.

PAYMENT CAP SYSTEM (EFFECTIVE MARCH 2026)
Pharmacy First caps are based on actual service delivery. Caps are calculated based on a monthly allocation and updated each month to reflect latest actual delivery. Activity can be delivered above the monthly cap but the £17 per consultation service fee will NOT be paid for consultations exceeding the cap.

March 2026 Banding:
- Band 1: Average <18 consultations/month → Cap at 45 consultations
- Band 2: Average 18-25 → Cap at 75
- Band 3: Average 26-31 → Cap at 100
- Band 4: Average 32-37 → Cap at 119
- Band 5: Average 38-46 → Cap at 144
- Band 6: Average 47+ → Cap at 234

Bands are calculated from average delivery October-December 2025.

KEY IMPLICATIONS
- Pharmacies delivering above their monthly cap will effectively provide NHS clinical consultations for free
- Higher-performing pharmacies are penalised for growing their Pharmacy First service
- The cap system creates a financial ceiling on pharmacy revenue from clinical services
- Pharmacies cannot easily move between bands as bands are recalculated monthly based on rolling 3-month averages
- The service fee increased from £15 to £17 per consultation in the 2025/26 settlement, but the cap limits total revenue

HISTORICAL CONTEXT
- Payment caps were first introduced in October 2024
- Caps have been tightened progressively each month
- The March 2026 caps represent the most restrictive banding yet applied
- Total funding for community pharmacy 2025/26: £3.073 billion baseline plus £215 million for Pharmacy First
