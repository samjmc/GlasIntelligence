CPCF FUNDING SETTLEMENT 2025/26
Source: GOV.uk, Community Pharmacy England (CPE)

BASELINE FUNDING
2025/26 baseline: £3.073 billion. This represents a 19.7% increase on 2024/25. Additional £215 million allocated for Pharmacy First and the Primary Care Recovery Plan. Total uplift versus 2023/24: greater than 30%. Minister Stephen Kinnock (Labour) described this as "the highest uplift awarded to any part of the NHS."

MEDICINES MARGIN AND FEES
Medicines margin for 2025/26: £900 million. Single Activity Fee increased by 19p to £1.46 per item from April 2025. £193 million write-off of historic margin overspend agreed.

CPE RESPONSE
Community Pharmacy England "reluctantly accepted" the settlement with "very serious caveats." Janet Morrison, CEO of CPE, stated: "We came to these negotiations as a sector in crisis -- with the impact of a decade's worth of real-terms cuts." Fin McCaul (CPE Negotiating Team) called it "a first step in the right direction." Peter Cattee (CPE Negotiating Team) emphasised the priority of getting money to pharmacy owners. Stephen Thomas (CCA representative) warned that walking away would risk "more than £800 million in extra funding."

BUNDLING REQUIREMENTS
Pharmacies must provide Hypertension Case-Finding and Pharmacy Contraception services to receive the Pharmacy First monthly payment. From October 2025, pharmacies must deliver at least one ABPM (ambulatory blood pressure monitoring) per month.

FEE STRUCTURE (PER CONSULTATION/SERVICE)
Minor illness (Pharmacy First): £17
Urgent medicine supply: £15
BP check: £10
ABPM: up to £50.85
Contraception: £25
Emergency hormonal contraception (EHC): £20
New Medicine Service (NMS): £14

CONTEXT
The settlement followed protracted negotiations. CPE had warned that the sector was in crisis after years of real-terms cuts. The bundling of Pharmacy First with Hypertension Case-Finding and Pharmacy Contraception was controversial, as some smaller or rural pharmacies argued they lacked capacity to deliver all three. The ABPM requirement from October 2025 adds further operational pressure.
