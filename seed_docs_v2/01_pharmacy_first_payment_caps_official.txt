NHSBSA PHARMACY FIRST PAYMENT CAP STRUCTURE
Source: NHSBSA (NHS Business Services Authority)

BAND SYSTEM OVERVIEW
Pharmacy First payments are capped using a 6-band structure. Each pharmacy is assigned to a band based on its rolling 3-month average delivery volume. Caps are recalculated monthly. Activity delivered above the cap is still provided to patients, but the £17 per consultation service fee is NOT paid for consultations exceeding the cap.

BAND THRESHOLDS AND MARCH 2026 CAPS
Band 1: Less than 18 consultations per month average. Cap: 45 consultations per month.
Band 2: 18 to 25 consultations per month average. Cap: 75 consultations per month.
Band 3: 26 to 31 consultations per month average. Cap: 100 consultations per month.
Band 4: 32 to 37 consultations per month average. Cap: 119 consultations per month.
Band 5: 38 to 46 consultations per month average. Cap: 144 consultations per month.
Band 6: 47 or more consultations per month average. Cap: 234 consultations per month.

CLINICAL PATHWAYS (7 CONDITIONS)
Pharmacy First covers seven clinical pathways: UTIs in women aged 16-64, shingles, impetigo, infected insect bites, earache in children aged 1-17, sore throat, and sinusitis.

THRESHOLD ESCALATION HISTORY
The consultation threshold for receiving monthly payments has risen repeatedly since launch:
February 2024: 1 consultation required
April 2024: 10 consultations required
June 2024: 20 consultations required
January 2025: 25 consultations required
March 2025: 30 consultations required

CAP INCREASES
January 2025 caps were increased by 55% to 94% over the October 2024 caps, reflecting pressure from the sector.

RECALCULATION
Band assignment and caps are recalculated each month based on the pharmacy's rolling 3-month average delivery. This creates volatility for pharmacies whose volumes fluctuate. A pharmacy delivering 20 consultations in month one, 15 in month two, and 25 in month three would have a rolling average of 20 and be assigned to Band 2 (cap 75). If the next month it delivers 40, the average rises and it may move to a higher band the following month.

CRITICISM
Sector bodies have argued that the cap structure punishes high-performing pharmacies and creates a perverse incentive to limit delivery. The £17 fee applies only to consultations within the cap; above-cap consultations generate no revenue despite requiring the same clinical time and resources.
